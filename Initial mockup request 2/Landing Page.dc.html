How a session goes

Eight minutes, four beats, no homework.

01 She opens

She calls out something from last time — the dog, the cat, your unreliable neighbour. No blank page.

02 You talk

Mic open the whole time. Fumble, and she asks you to say it again — she genuinely didn't catch it.

03 She tells you

Three patterns, ranked by what got in the way of being understood — plus what you steered around.

04 Tomorrow

Same scene, one notch harder — the cat's ill, and she wants it in the tense you avoided today.

The one real decision

Your mic never closes.

No button to hold before you're allowed to speak. Interrupt her and she stops. Say пра́вда? mid-story and she hears it and carries on.
